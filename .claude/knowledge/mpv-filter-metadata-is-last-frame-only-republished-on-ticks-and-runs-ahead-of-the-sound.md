## mpv's `af-metadata` is the last filtered frame only, republished on ticks, and runs ~230 ms ahead of the sound
**Area:** projection
**Trigger:** touching the engine's level meter (`meterLoop` in `native/engine/engine.cpp`, the `level` event) or reading any per-frame value out of libmpv's filters

- **Observing `af-metadata/<label>` loses most frames.** mpv keeps only the LAST filtered frame's metadata (`f_lavfi.c` copies it per frame) and observers are re-polled only on core ticks: `handle_dummy_ticks` (`player/playloop.c`) every 50 ms at best for audio-only, and much slower in practice. Measured 2026-11-15 (darwin engine, 1 kHz tone): a WAV gave ~13 updates/s, an AAC song one every ~500 ms. And an observer is notified only on a CHANGE: with `asetnsamples=n=1920` a steady tone makes every 40 ms frame identical, and the levels stopped after the first one.
- **What works:** a thread of the engine's own polls `mpv_get_property(..., "af-metadata/meter", MPV_FORMAT_NODE)` every 20 ms over fixed 40 ms frames (`af=@meter:lavfi=[asetnsamples=n=1920:p=0,astats=metadata=1:reset=1]`): a level every 40 ms on Linux, ~41 on macOS, ~46 on Windows; -12.0 dBFS read exactly for a -12 tone on all three; same at mpv volume 100 and 50 (the filter sees the sound before mpv's gain, which the AO applies). FFmpeg 4.4 / mpv 0.34 (Ubuntu 22.04) has both filters, the same keys (`lavfi.astats.1.Peak_level`) and `measure_perchannel` (an earlier note here said it had not: checked in that image 2026-11-15, `measure_perchannel=Peak_level:measure_overall=none` parses and leaves only the Peak_level keys). With it a 600 s AAC costs 0.78 s of CPU against 3.55 s for the full `astats` (0.58 decoding alone, M3). A filter libavfilter refuses fails the audio chain at the file's start («Audio filter initialized failed!»): the engine then plays the file again with the full one. 1920 samples are 10 ms at 192 kHz, and polled every 20 ms most of them went unread (2 of ~27 short bursts seen): a file above 48 kHz gets frames of 40 ms at its own rate (`af` set at FILE_LOADED), all bursts seen. With no meter while it should play (its track dropped) or on mpv's null output, the level is -120, never the last one frozen. `astats` writes `-inf` for digital silence (not JSON: the engine clamps to -120).
- **The filter runs when mpv fills its output buffer, not when the room hears it:** 0.2 s of `audio-buffer` plus the system's latency. Measured with a tone that starts at 2.000 s against mpv's `time-pos`: ~230 ms early on macOS (Core Audio), Windows (WASAPI) and Linux's speakers (PipeWire; a null sink ~290 ms). The engine holds each reading `METER_LEAD_MS` (230) before emitting it; after that the meter is within -59…+32 ms (Mac), -37…+10 (Windows), -27…-10 (Linux speakers). A Bluetooth output (slower) still shows its level early. A seek clears the held readings.
