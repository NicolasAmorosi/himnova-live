## Engine frames drawn as they land beat against every compositor — paced by rAF NDI is clean, but a late main frame still drops 60 fps on the projector
**Area:** projection
**Trigger:** touching `EngineVideo` in `output-view.tsx`, `lib/frame-pacer.ts`, or any path that puts media-engine frames on a page; measuring engine-video smoothness

- Until 2026-11-10 each engine frame was `drawImage`d into its canvas the moment it arrived. The 60 Hz projector and the 30 Hz offscreen NDI window then took the canvas on their own clocks: by the phase each playback started with (fixed for the whole play), NDI lost 8–37 % of a 30 fps engine video in 4 starts out of 12 and a 1080p60 lost 0.1–45 % on the projector — the 2.7.0 NDI bug's class. `phasehist.py` (review folder) separated it perfectly: loss iff the draws fell 6–18 ms after the NDI window's rAF.
- Fix: copy each frame into a small `OffscreenCanvas` queue as it lands (the engine's texture goes back at once — its ring has 6 slots and three windows hold frames) and put one up per `requestAnimationFrame` with `FramePacer`: `floor(frameInterval / refreshInterval + ¼)` refreshes per frame, both intervals measured, the oldest at once when two wait. Measured after (barcode videos, ScreenCaptureKit on the real screen, an NDI receiver, 6 starts each): NDI 0 % lost in every start, 30 fps 0 lost on screen.
- **What is left, and why:** ~1 main frame in 5 Chromium starts its `requestAnimationFrame` ~8 ms late (no long task, no arrival nearby — its own scheduling); a canvas updated in such a frame misses that refresh. 30 fps keeps every frame but alternates 17/50 ms on ~25 % of them; 60 fps loses 0.3–20 % on the Mac's screen. Measured and rejected: `desynchronized: true` (no change), a worker-owned `OffscreenCanvas` (23–27 % lost at 60 fps), a `MediaStreamTrackGenerator` `<video>` fed on arrival (25–31 %) or paced (same 25 % unevenness). The way out would be a presentation path that is not the page's main frame.
- **Since 2026-11-10 a source faster than 40 fps goes up at half its rate** (owner: «a 30, en los 3 sistemas»): `FramePacer.arrived` keeps every other frame, `tick` holds each two refreshes. Measured on the Mac's screen with a 1080p60 `.ts`, 3 starts: exactly every other frame shown, 33 ms each, 0–2 frames lost that the page never got either (were 1.7–13 % at random). A `<video>` keeps its 60.
- Measure on the screen, never in the page: `screencapture -v` loses 1.5–32 % on its own; the review's `screenbar` (ScreenCaptureKit strip, barcode per refresh) and `native/ndi-probe count` are the instruments. Run ≥ 6 starts: the loss lives in each start's phase.

**Last updated:** 2026-11-10 (60 fps halved)
