## NDI 1080p30 does not fit a 100 Mbps link (it collapses, not degrades), and a receiver may connect through a VPN's address
**Area:** projection
**Trigger:** a report of a stuttering or freezing NDI output, writing the NDI guide or Settings texts, or choosing NDI defaults (resolution, frame rate, transport)

- **Bitrate, measured over loopback** (no link limiting it; 2026-11-09, NDI 6.3, BGRX in): 1080p30 **≈ 106 Mbps** for detailed footage (a 4K marathon crowd), ≈ 76 for a music video; 720p30 ≈ 68 and ≈ 41. NDI publishes no 1080p30 row; its 1080i60 (same pixel rate) maximum is 112.5 Mbps. So a **100 Mbps link (≈ 94 usable) cannot carry 1080p30 NDI**, and 60 fps doubles it.
- **How it fails on such a link** (Mac on Wi-Fi → Windows whose NIC auto-negotiated 100 Mbps, one receiver): with the default reliable UDP the receiver got 22–25 fps for **3 seconds and then nothing** for the rest of the run; forced to TCP (`ndi-config.v1.json` with only `tcp` enabled, via `NDI_CONFIG_DIR`) it never stopped but saturated at 94.5 Mbps and lost 16–21 %. A second receiver on the same PC (OBS + a probe) starved one of them to ~2 fps. At 720p everything fit. The SDK has no bitrate knob (NDI|HX needs the Advanced SDK), so the only levers are resolution, frame rate and the network: the Settings texts and the guide say "1080p: gigabit cable; Wi-Fi or 100 Mbps: 720p".
- **VPN adapters** (ZeroTier there, same for Tailscale): the sender is announced on every interface and the **receiver picks an address** — OBS/DistroAV connected through ZeroTier's `10.x` address, reconnecting every ~3 s, and a probe found by name went through it in one run and through the LAN in the next. Restricting the sender (`"adapters": {"allowed": [<LAN IP>]}` in the engine's config) did **not** change what OBS picked; the same key on the receiver's side kept it on the LAN in 1 of 2 tries. ZeroTier alone carried 720p fine (0.2 % lost): it adds risk, it was not the whole problem. DistroAV's own troubleshooting says no VPN.
- A Mac on Wi-Fi 5 showed 11 ms average / 90 ms peak ping to the PC at rest; Windows → Mac at 720p lost ~7 % in single frames while Mac → Windows lost 0–0.2 % (inferred: the Mac's Wi-Fi). NDI asks for wired gigabit (Wi-Fi 6/7 at least).

**Last updated:** 2026-11-09
