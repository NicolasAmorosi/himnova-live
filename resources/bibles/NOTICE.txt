Lumina Live ships these Bible versions, public-domain or freely licensed classics. A version is copied
into the app's own database when it is installed (from Settings, or at first launch for the versions in
the interface's language); the files here are never changed.

Each file keeps the verse text of the 66 books and nothing else, every verse under its source's own
number (a verse a source leaves out is absent, never renumbered): footnotes, cross references, section
headings, psalm superscriptions marked as headings, Strong's numbers and paragraph marks were left out.

Almeida 1911 (almeida1911.xml), Portuguese
  The text is in the public domain. Its digitization is the CrossWire Bible Society's module
  PorAlmeida1911 (version 0.2.1), distributed under the GNU General Public License.
  Source: https://crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=PorAlmeida1911
  Changes: 75 OCR artefacts, a "*" left where a printed line broke, corrected by hand (the word joined,
  or its hyphen kept, as this same text spells it elsewhere), three stray punctuation marks removed,
  and the space before punctuation removed in 18 verses. The 1911 spelling is kept.
